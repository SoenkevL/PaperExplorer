The eval does not meet its bar, so point 4 of the asking work's definition of done is not reached, and nothing in `asking` was changed. It misses on Pointer coverage alone, and by more than in run 3: 0.69 against 0.797. The cause is the redraft: it was asked for 18 times and went through 7 times; the 11 that failed left the only live Answers without a single Pointer, and those hold 32 of the 60 unpointed sentences (below). **How the Answers were judged**, as in stages 8b and 9b (the verdicts file says it too). *Grounded*: every claim is borne out by the Seed, by the place a Pointer leads to, else by what the model read (the whole Seed, live); one claim the Seed does not make or contradicts makes the Answer ungrounded; a true unpointed sentence is left to Pointer coverage. Saying "the Seed does not say" of a covered point is such a claim. *Correct*: the substance on the point asked is right; an Answer to another Question, or one that misses the point, is not. A failed turn is neither. A redrafted Answer is judged as shown, the redraft. **Where parsing lost words, the Answer is judged by the Chunk** as stored, which is what the model read, not by the printed page: the user's rule, settled after 2026-10-29 (2.5, pages 5-6). This run it decided two kinds of reading: six Answers on intensity ratings (live, all three repeats, and retrieval) credit the eyes-closed association to the low-dose LSD study, as the Chunk reads past its gap, and that is not held against them; their Pointers to that sentence count as apt. Read strictly, so that a grounded and correct Answer must also point every substantive sentence (uncovered Questions excepted), live scores 30 of 66 (11, 10, 9 per repeat) and retrieval 10 of 22 (run 3: live 11 of 22, retrieval 4).

## What each change did

The drafts as the model wrote them, before the cut and with their handles, are in the traces; all 88 turns' observations were read after the run, with each redraft's request and its first draft.

- **The Question last.** No live Answer answered another Question (run 3: 2 of 22, `7d24a397f47c1f7f9bc439661c95970e` and `35e1371558966e774f2a2d9466d2bb4f`). Both of run 3's Questions were answered on the point in all three repeats. And no first request called a tool, 0 of 66, so ending on the Question did not bring back the tool calls that once made 8b put the no-tools note last. A new slip came instead: four live Answers to covered Questions open with "the Seed does not say" and then answer anyway (pattern 1). The closing turn now puts the prompt's line on what to do where the Seed does not settle a Question right before the Question, which may invite it; run 3's Answers to the same Questions did not do this.
- **The redraft**, asked for 18 times (6, 7 and 5 per repeat; 27% of live turns, against run 3's 3 handle-less Answers of 22).
  - **7 went through**, and each pointed every one of its 22 substantive sentences. Six repeat the first draft word for word; one (`2376f2341183012b2d229325e4c849b3`, `method-sample-entropy-parameters`, repeat 2) reworded and split its second sentence and dropped a figure's name, with no claim changed. No redraft changed a claim.
  - **11 failed**, each ending at pydantic-ai's limit of one request, after which the first draft stood. In 8 the model answered the redraft with a call to a tool that does not exist (six to `Seed`, one each to two longer invented names); in 2 of those the call's arguments held the redrafted Answer with its handles (`f04ca5ddbc2986c812e15cc00610e494`, `57384824a3ae2b92f11110e1426b049b`). In 3 the model sent back nothing (`050caf0b66ad77ccf5ed611318b8e435`, `c88aab7afb5b7356d5fb64651354672c`, `8c88b5879299f3eb98b5867ad2b8008c`). Either way pydantic-ai wants a second request, to say the tool is unknown or to ask for text, and the limit refuses it.
  - **Coverage:** the 11 failures are exactly the 11 live Answers with no Pointer, 32 of the 60 unpointed sentences. Had each pointed all its sentences, live coverage would be 0.86 (168 of 196); without the 7 that went through, it would be 0.58.
  - **Cost:** redrafted turns took 21.1 s on average, failed ones 14.9 s, the rest 8.5 s. A failed redraft's request is not counted in `model_requests`, but its invented tool is: the 8 calls are live's 0.12 tool calls per turn in the summary, and the stream sent each as a `step`, so the sidebar would show a step for a tool that does not exist before the first draft returns.
- **Bare `[n]`**: no draft of either mode, first or redrafted, wrote a bare bracketed number (run 3's live app did, `dc485d10c4cd2e5d03a5901b88429045`). The reading had nothing to read in this run; it is shown only by its tests. Of 203 live handle groups, 202 are `[#n]` and one `[#n,n]`; retrieval wrote 75, all `[#n]`.
- **Thinking off.** None of the 84 live generations (66 answering requests and 18 redrafts) returned a thinking part, nor did retrieval's four sixth requests; retrieval's 56 requests that offer tools all thought, as designed. Live's first draft text now comes after 1.1 s at the median (run 3: 11.1 s). Whether thinking off is also why more first drafts carry no handle cannot be told from this run, since every change acts at once (below).
- **Temperature 0.5**: see retrieval, above. Live's three repeats agree within 3.6 points of coverage and one Answer of the judged line.
- **Control tokens and the cut:** none of the 88 first drafts or the 7 redrafts holds a control token or a `<system>` block, so the cut changed no Answer; no turn failed, and none ended with "The model gave no Answer.".

## The Support threshold

`PE_SUPPORT_THRESHOLD` stays **0.70**. Every Pointer of the run was read against the Seed (168 live, 65 retrieval) as in 8b: *apt*, the place says what the sentence says; *partly*, it bears out part of it (often one clause of a sentence that has a Pointer per clause); *not*, it does not bear it out.

| Cosine | Run 4 live: apt / partly / not | Run 4 retrieval: apt / partly / not | Run 4 | Runs 1-4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ≥ 0.75 | 95 / 46 / 0 | 36 / 21 / 0 | 198, 0 inapt | 416, 1 inapt |
| 0.70-0.75 | 2 / 19 / 1 | 1 / 5 / 0 | 28, 1 inapt | 85, 6 inapt |
| 0.60-0.70 | 2 / 1 / 0 | 2 / 0 / 0 | 5, 0 inapt | 27, 3 inapt |
| < 0.60 | 2 / 0 / 0 | - | 2, 0 inapt | 20, 9 inapt |

- At or above 0.70, 226 Pointers, of which 1 is inapt: a sentence that reverses the Seed, pointed at the place it reverses (0.731, `817d9ed9f87a52228e1d905980a0ebd0`). Below 0.70, 7, all apt or partly: two are an Answer's count of studies, pointed at a sentence that gives the count among other things (0.600).
- Over all four runs: 501 Pointers at or above 0.70 with 7 inapt (1.4%), 47 below with 12 inapt (26%).
- Raising the threshold to 0.75 would make 27 apt or partly apt Pointers of this run's 0.70-0.75 band weak, to catch 1. Lowering it to 0.60 would cost nothing in this run (5 Pointers, all apt or partly) but would have called 3 inapt Pointers close in runs 1-3.
- Whole Seed made far fewer weak Pointers than in run 3 (5 of 168 against 14 of 70), and more of its Pointers sit on an expected place (0.80 against 0.70).

## Failure patterns

Each pattern names the traces that show it (Langfuse, `eval` environment).

1. **"The Seed does not say" on a covered Question.** Four live Answers open with the prescribed form for an uncovered Question and then answer: `shortcoming-specificity` in every repeat (`bd88fe0b39f7c59c57f1feaf6bbf8a36`, `817d9ed9f87a52228e1d905980a0ebd0`, `ef4efe85e973e798bc33370a3645b76d`) and `shortcoming-own-scope` in repeat 3 (`e8814a8921f1513251cd4fc882005e0e`). New in this run; run 3's live Answers to both were right. It counts against grounding, and for specificity against correctness too, since the Answer never says whether the rise is specific.
2. **Claims the Seed does not make.** Live has seven such Answers, retrieval one:
   - reversed: entropy said to rise in deep sleep and anesthesia (`817d9ed9f87a52228e1d905980a0ebd0`), fMRI's resolution turned from a possible strength into a limitation (`c134ba40c6323b0c18815f749ff1eee3`), spatial working memory said to be impaired (`de91b3220a9a9417dc6297e1f70e8a06`);
   - a study's metric given wrongly, the connectome-harmonics study as temporal LZC (`e3dd65cd5506fd01c4a6166d97f34fc8`, `02dff7f4c0e86cfb14fad056a837f902`);
   - a group swapped: patients with disorders of consciousness among the states where entropy rises, where the Seed names psychiatric disorders (`b79ebe5498d8a8065d4a14c3f007e90f`);
   - narrowed: psilocybin trials for depression, where the Seed speaks of psychedelics (`1a49f74ed9bd3b086ea1bf084dd89ee0`, uncertain);
   - retrieval, an inferred recommendation (`be41918ca8692b675066f1af6bfb10f3`).
3. **Answers without handles, and the redraft that failed.** 18 live first drafts carried no handle; 11 redrafts failed on a tool that does not exist or an empty response (the change list above names them). Retrieval had no Answer without handles.
4. **Unpointed opening lines.** Of the 28 unpointed sentences in Answers that do carry Pointers, 21 are the Answer's first sentence, most announcing what follows, such as a count of themes or proposals, and four of them pattern 1's openers. The other 7 sit mid-Answer, four of them in `measurement-tagliazucchi-motifs`, whose Answers put one handle after a paragraph of four sentences.
5. **Tools that do not exist**, now in whole-Seed redrafts (pattern 3); retrieval called none (run 3: 3 turns).
6. **Answering another Question** (retrieval). `e68555de8b855cf262f6942e4be51298` lists the field's open problems for what the review leaves out, as run 3's `448b0aa1b3b3490eb24f1a310c12392f` did.

## What would raise the failing number

**Pointer coverage (0.69).** Each lever is a proposal for the user; none was built.
- **Let the redraft survive a tool call or an empty response.** Had the 11 failed redrafts pointed their sentences as the 7 others did, coverage would be 0.86. Three ways, to measure with the eval:
  - give the redraft a second request, so pydantic-ai's retry (the tool is unknown, or text is wanted) can be answered; the model then sees its slip named, and the redraft costs one more request when it slips;
  - read the redraft's tool-call arguments when they hold handled text, as 2 of the 8 calls did; narrow, and it makes text in a tool call count as an Answer, which ADR 0008's line between the model's words and the authors' does not forbid but which the cut and the tool-name withholding were built around;
  - another model, as before: this fine-tune calls tools nobody offered whenever a request looks like a task, which is now the redraft's main failure.
- **Find out why first drafts lost their handles.** 27% of live first drafts had none, against 14% in run 3. Thinking off, the Question last and the temperature changed together. A replay of the six Questions most affected (`method-lzc-vs-shannon`, `method-sample-entropy-parameters`, `measurement-intensity-correlation`, `measurement-mcculloch-replication`, `measurement-table-1-studies`, `method-study-selection`), live ×3 with thinking on and with it off at temperature 0.5, would separate thinking from the rest in about 10 minutes of the tower's time.
- **Settle whether an opening line needs a Pointer.** 21 of the 28 other unpointed sentences open an Answer, most announcing a list. Leaving such a line out of the measure is a choice about the measure, the user's, not about the Answers; with every first sentence of a pointed Answer left out, this run's coverage would be 0.78 (136 of 175).
- **Pattern 1** costs grounding, not coverage, on the judged line. Rewording the closing turn's line so the "does not say" form is for a Question the Seed does not take up at all, rather than for any part it does not settle, would aim at it; it changes the prompt's version and needs a run.

**Judged grounded and correct (85%)** passes in every repeat. Its misses are patterns 1 and 2. Pattern 1 is the one with a likely cause in this stage's changes.
