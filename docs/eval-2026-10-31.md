# Eval of asking the Seed, 2026-10-31

The first eval with whole-Seed answering live (ADR 0010), judged. The 22 Questions about the Seed were each asked twice, in fresh Conversations: *live*, as the reader asks (`auto`, which read the whole Seed on all 22 turns), and by *retrieval*, forced, the fallback for Seeds that do not fit. Every Answer was read against the Seed's 105 Chunks. This report holds metrics, places, verdicts, trace ids and observations only: no words of the Seed or of the Answers, whose licence is unknown. The full report, which quotes both, stays in `data/eval/<run id>/`.

The run ran on the tower with `paperexplorer-chat` (`num_ctx` 73728), bge-m3, Support threshold 0.70 and the same 22 Questions as before (`seed-questions.yaml`, sha256 `e498dca4d79c`; 17 covered, 5 uncovered). The Seed's Chunks hold 21,829 bge-m3 tokens, inside the 40,000 budget. Traces are in Langfuse's `eval` environment, tagged `run:<run id>`, the live ones also `whole-seed`.

**How the Answers were judged**, as in stage 8b (the verdicts file says it too). *Grounded*: every claim is borne out by the Seed, by the place a Pointer leads to, else by what the model read (the whole Seed, live); one claim the Seed does not make or contradicts makes the Answer ungrounded; a true unpointed sentence is left to Pointer coverage. *Correct*: the substance on the point asked is right; an Answer to another Question, or one that misses the point, is not. A failed turn is neither. **Where parsing lost words, the Answer is judged by the Chunk** as stored, which is what the model read, not by the printed page: the user settled this rule after 2026-10-29 (2.5, pages 5-6). In this run the rule changed no verdict: only one Answer drew on the words around the gap (retrieval, `b2b90b56e2aaf622db5247a90889536e`), and its count is wrong by the Chunk as well. Read strictly, so that a grounded and correct Answer must also point every substantive sentence (uncovered Questions excepted), live scores 11 of 22 and retrieval 4 (run 2: baseline 12, retrieval 7).

## Handles and the control-token cut

What the two fixes of PR #18 were for, with what this run's files show. The drafts as the model wrote them, before the cut and with their handles, are in the traces: the agent run's output, recorded before the cut. All 44 were read after the run.

- **Unpointed sentences, live: 14 of 69** in covered Answers, against 27 of 81 in run 2's baseline.
  - 7 of them sit in three Answers with no handle of any form left in the text: `7f32813a3ef4cd52aca983d0e0ad660e` (3), `70cf08933dac78bcf79873edbada5a1e` (2) and `35e1371558966e774f2a2d9466d2bb4f` (2). Coverage is still all or nothing per Answer (run 2's baseline: 3 such Answers).
  - The other 7 sit in six Answers. Three of them open an Answer: two announce a list, one is a bare no.
- **Unpointed sentences, retrieval: 26 of 64.** 19 of them sit in four Answers: author-and-year citations in place of handles (`529c1efd58b0d555a47fed3d61694cad`, 6), groups without a `#`, such as bracketed dashes, bare numbers and a percent sign, which the grammar rightly leaves as text, so the reader sees them (`b2b90b56e2aaf622db5247a90889536e`, 5; `cd0a43825d2c772f90a951e2bcee2aec`, 4), and none at all (`65bbb14ad4b2ae1a0b7b40c537e8a182`, 4).
- **No handle form with a `#` was left unread**: no Answer's text holds a bracketed group with a `#`, and no handle was unknown. The drafts hold 78 handle groups with a `#` in live and 68 in retrieval. Of them, 5 live groups in five Answers (`[#n,n]`, `[#n,n,n,n]`, `[#n##n]`, a range `[#n-n]`, `{#n}`) and 4 retrieval groups in one Answer (`(#n)` three times, a range `(#n-#n)`) are forms only PR #18 reads: before it they would have stayed in the text, and their sentences would have lost those Pointers.
- **Forms without a `#`** are the gap now. Besides the two retrieval Answers above, the live app's check Question (below) was answered with bare bracketed Chunk numbers and so got no Pointer at all; this Seed prints no numbered citation markers, so there they could only mean Chunks.
- **Control tokens: none in any of the 44 Answers**, and no echoed `<system>` (run 2: two baseline Answers trailed tokens and monologue; run 1: one Answer was nothing but a token). **Answers failing with no words left: 0**: no turn ended with "The model gave no Answer.". **Answers cut: 0**: none of the 44 drafts holds a control token or a `<system>` block, so the cut had nothing to do in this run. Whether it works on a real leak is still shown only by its regression tests.
- One leak remains outside the Answer. In retrieval `448b0aa1b3b3490eb24f1a310c12392f` the model sent a tool call whose name held a drafted Answer with `<channel|>` and `<|tool_call>` in it; pydantic-ai refused the unknown tool and the turn went on to answer. The cut reads the draft, not tool names, and the sidebar shows the tools a turn runs as its steps. Whole-Seed turns have no tools, so live is not exposed.
  - The eval's summary records tool names as they come, so this one carried the model's drafted words into `evals/runs/<run id>.json`, which is meant to hold none. In the committed copy that one name is withheld, marked as such; nothing else differs from the summary as written. ## The Support threshold

`PE_SUPPORT_THRESHOLD` stays **0.70**. Every Pointer of the run was read against the Seed (70 live, 57 retrieval) as in 8b: *apt*, the place says what the sentence says; *partly*, it bears out part of it (typically one item of a list sentence); *not*, it does not bear it out.

- At or above 0.70, 107 Pointers, of which 4 are inapt: an opening line of the Answer to another Question (0.755, `7d24a397f47c1f7f9bc439661c95970e`), and three in the 0.70-0.75 band, each a general sentence Pointed at a nearby but different claim (`0a1dcf0a0cce7c755a21fb44cd992253`, `5441106895b79ca0232cc72b547418d0`, `dcf10ce6ce7c0d60cff3d0d9bbefe494`). Below 0.70, 20, of which 5 are inapt.
- Over all three runs: 275 Pointers at or above 0.70 with 6 inapt (2%), 40 below with 12 inapt (30%).
- Raising the threshold to 0.75 would make the 15 apt or partly apt Pointers of this run's 0.70-0.75 band weak, to catch 3. Lowering it to 0.65 would call this run's one inapt Pointer of 0.65-0.70 close (0.686, `46bcc12115b7f43324c93fba62da118a`, a sentence that contradicts the Seed) along with 5 apt or partly apt ones.

## Failure patterns

Each pattern names the traces that show it (Langfuse, `eval` environment).

1. **Answering another Question, or missing it.** Live twice: `7d24a397f47c1f7f9bc439661c95970e` (how the review argues, asked about one study's motifs) and `35e1371558966e774f2a2d9466d2bb4f` (says the Seed does not cover the limitations it is about). Retrieval three times: `ee7858a278be0a413669d9f7feea13ef`, `9c3b5f44ea793320d487b8299ea945d0` (as in run 2 on the same Question, `998814d78c81219e1af98cfb26307784`), `448b0aa1b3b3490eb24f1a310c12392f`. Run 2's baseline, the same request as live, had none. The live pair's thinking shows the same slip.
   - In `35e1371558966e774f2a2d9466d2bb4f` the model takes the closing no-tools note for the reader's message, finds no Question in it, and says the Seed does not cover it.
   - In `7d24a397f47c1f7f9bc439661c95970e` it restates the Question as two other tasks before it answers.
   - In the whole-Seed request, that note follows the Question and reaches the model as a user turn of its own in `<system>` tags (`_NoteAfterReading`). So the last user turn the model reads is the note, not the Question. Stage 8b put the note last because placed earlier the model ignored it and called tools. A change must keep the note at the end and the Question with it, for example by folding the note into the Question's own turn, and be measured for both (below).
2. **Claims the Seed does not make.** Live has four such Answers, retrieval eleven.
   - Invented numbers: `d19a587309344cfc34ec8e556057c8c6` (table rows), `b2598e717a40352759a7ef7d03898af7` (studies reviewed), `b2b90b56e2aaf622db5247a90889536e` (positive studies).
   - A finding given to the wrong study: `8529174062cacd6cb1c39e7e3ecd14f7`, `529c1efd58b0d555a47fed3d61694cad`; a drug and an effect invented for a cited pilot: `7d24a397f47c1f7f9bc439661c95970e`.
   - Reversed: equal entropy read as higher (`46bcc12115b7f43324c93fba62da118a`), short timescales read as long (`65bbb14ad4b2ae1a0b7b40c537e8a182`), lower alertness read as raising entropy (`38429ae64a922c0d67a226fc26a161fb`).
   - Invented recommendations or claims of scope: `035924d9f630840981a3db55155a17c5`, `9a615df52a3afa87f8122089feb1a81f`, `8aacaad17236567b2aed381777998409`; an abbreviation spelt out wrong: `448b0aa1b3b3490eb24f1a310c12392f`.
   - A claim about the text itself: `d1894864192385e69771a94020624b91` says a section is missing from the whole Seed it was given.
3. **Answers without handles, or with handles without a `#`.** Live: `7f32813a3ef4cd52aca983d0e0ad660e`, `70cf08933dac78bcf79873edbada5a1e`, `35e1371558966e774f2a2d9466d2bb4f` carry none. In the live app's check, bare Chunk numbers (`dc485d10c4cd2e5d03a5901b88429045`, environment `tower`).

7. **Garbled words and other languages**, as listed under the cut above; noted, not counted against an Answer.

## What would raise the failing numbers

- **Pointer coverage (0.797, one sentence short).** Half the gap is three Answers with no handle at all, so the two levers from 2026-10-29 that were not built stand:
  - When a covered whole-Seed Answer has substantive sentences and no handle, ask once more for the handles. It costs one more request, with the Seed already in Ollama's prompt cache. Had it pointed every sentence of those three Answers, live coverage would be 62 of 69, 0.90.
  - Read a bare bracketed number as a handle when the Seed prints no numbered citation markers, as this Seed does (it cites by author and year). That changes ADR 0008's rule that `[12]` stays the Seed's own marker, so it is a decision; it would have pointed the live app's Answer.
  - Of the other seven unpointed sentences, three open an Answer (two announce a list, one is a bare no). Whether such a line needs a Pointer is a question for the measure, not the Answers; worth settling before tuning towards 0.80.
- **Judged grounded and correct (82%, met by one Answer).** The misses are drifting to another Question and invented claims (patterns 1, 2), not reading: live read every word. Levers, each to measure with the eval:
  - the no-tools note and the Question in one closing turn of the whole-Seed request, rather than the note as a turn of its own after the Question. Both live Answers to another Question lost the Question behind that note (pattern 1). The eval must show that tool calls stay away, which is why 8b put the note last. It changes the prompt's version;
  - a lower sampling temperature, since the Modelfile sets none. It would also narrow the run-to-run spread that took retrieval from 68% to 36% with nothing in it changed that could;
  - another model, as on 2026-10-29: this fine-tune still calls tools nobody offered and writes handle forms of its own;
  - repeating each Question two or three times per run, so that a change of a few points can be told from the spread.
- **Retrieval (36%)**, the fallback, is not on the bar, but it is what Sources will use. Its reading habit (pattern 5) and its invented claims are the same as on 2026-10-29; the unknown-tool failure (pattern 4) ended a turn again.
